Privacy Policy

App: Atmanirbhar · Developer: Uncle Sam Son LLC · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Independent app — not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Government of India.

In short: the app works on your device. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data. Your shop's bills, items and customer details stay on your phone or tablet unless you choose to export or share them.

1. What the app stores, and where

To run your shop's billing, the app saves on your device only:

  • Your shop details (name, address, phone, GSTIN, FSSAI number, UPI ID) that you enter.
  • Items, prices and stock.
  • Bills, credit notes, day-close records and udhaar (khata) entries.
  • Customer details you choose to add (name, mobile number, and optionally GSTIN, address, state, PAN), plus whether the customer agreed to receive bills on WhatsApp.

This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server run by us. The app has no account sign-up, no analytics, and no advertising.

2. When data leaves your device (only when you choose)

  • WhatsApp: when you tap WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp with the bill text or receipt image so you can send it. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to what you send.
  • Share / export: backups, CSV files (such as GSTR-1 data) and receipt images are handed to the app you pick in your phone's share menu (for example Google Drive, email or WhatsApp).
  • Printing: receipts go to the printer or print-service app you choose.
  • UPI: the app shows a UPI QR code with your UPI ID and the amount. The customer's UPI app handles the payment. The app never handles money, card numbers or UPI PINs.

3. Customers' personal data (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

As the shop owner, you decide which customer details to record and why, for example for udhaar or GST invoices. Please collect only what you need, and send WhatsApp messages only to customers who agree; the app asks you to confirm this. You can edit customer details at any time, and delete a customer who has no bills or khata history. Bills already issued must be kept for GST records (at least 72 months).

4. Permissions

The app uses internet access only to open links you tap (such as WhatsApp) and for the optional features described below, each of which you turn on yourself.

Version 1.0 only (licence codes). In version 1.0 of the app, entering a licence code downloads one small encrypted licence file once. That download sends no personal data — only a random file name derived from the code; the hosting provider may see your IP address, as with any web request. Later versions do not use licence codes.

Subscription. If you activate or connect your shop with an activation code from us, our server (in the United States) keeps your shop's subscription record: the shop name we noted when you subscribed, which features are included, the paid-until date and whether it is active. The app asks the server for this record so the right features switch on. It contains no bills, items or customer data.

If you turn on UPI auto-confirm, the app talks directly to your own Razorpay account to create a QR code for each bill (amount, shop name and bill number) and to check whether it has been paid. This goes straight from your phone to Razorpay under Razorpay's own privacy policy; it never passes through us. Your Razorpay API keys are stored encrypted on your device only. The same applies if you link a PhonePe card machine: the bill amount goes from your phone directly to PhonePe for your machine, and your PhonePe details stay encrypted on your device. It does not ask for your contacts, location, microphone or files.

Sync between your devices (optional). If you turn on Sync devices, your shop's data (items, bills, customers, khata and shop settings) is copied to a sync server operated by us in the United States so that your phone, tablet and PC show the same data. This means the encrypted copy is stored outside India. If your subscription includes syncing, it is switched on when you activate the app with your activation code. Before anything leaves your device it is encrypted with a key that exists only on your own devices; we cannot read it, and neither can anyone who gains access to the server. The server keeps the encrypted copy, its size, the times your devices sync and the internet address they connect from (kept in logs for 180 days as Indian rules require). You can delete the server copy at any time in Settings → Sync devices → Turn off → “delete the server copy”, or by writing to us.

Notification access (optional). If you turn on auto-confirm from your payment app, Android asks you to give the app Notification access. The app then reads only notifications from Google Pay, Google Pay for Business, PhonePe, PhonePe Business, Paytm, Paytm for Business and BHIM, to spot “money received” alerts and match the amount to the bill that is open. Notifications from every other app are ignored. The alerts it reads stay in the phone's memory for at most 30 minutes, are never saved to storage, and are never sent to us or anyone else. You can switch this off at any time in the app or in Android settings (Notifications → Notification access).

5. Children

The app is a business tool for shop owners and is not directed at children under 18.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all data stored by the app on that device. Export a backup first if you need your records.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions or grievances: support@unclesamson.com.
